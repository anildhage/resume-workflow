# Profit and Loss Reporting

**Organization:** Societe Generale Investment Banking  
**Period:** 01/2026 - 06/2026  
**Role:** Lead BA / data transformation developer

## Business context
- The work supported internal PMPI performance-management reporting for MARK, GLBA, and GTPS.
- Management needed daily, MTD, QTD, and YTD views of P&L and NBI performance versus budget to support timely decisions before month-end close.

## Problem or objective
- The reporting process needed repeatable, standardized metrics and daily aggregation across business units.
- The objective was to replace fragmented manual views with a consistent management-reporting model that surfaced variance and budget performance early.

## Responsibilities and contributions
- Translated management reporting requirements into data models, metric definitions, and report layouts.
- Owned the end-to-end P&L and NBI ETL transformations.
- Ingested daily feeds from MARK, GLBA, GTPS, and their sub-business units.
- Implemented cumulative calculations, average-balance logic, budget prorations, validations, and variance calculations.
- Checked feed completeness and timeliness.
- Handled late or missing submissions, reruns, corrections, new desks, and business restructures.
- Worked with a UI developer to maintain dashboard layouts and PPT outputs.
- Answered questions on variance versus prior periods or budget and adjusted logic where needed.

## Tools, platforms, and systems
- FARMS ETL
- SQL
- Python
- SG Markets and management-reporting channels
- Daily business-unit feeds and batch orchestration

## Outcomes and value
- Replaced fragmented manual views with repeatable daily reporting.
- Gave management clearer visibility into actual performance versus budget.
- Created earlier warning of under-performance.
- Standardized DTD, MTD, QTD, and YTD calculations across business units.
- Improved consistency and repeatability of management-reporting outputs.

## Evidence or summary
- “I led the BA and data-transformation work for a PMPI reporting solution covering MARK, GLBA, and GTPS. I designed the DTD, MTD, QTD, YTD, budget-proration, and variance logic, integrated daily business-unit feeds, and partnered with the UI developer on Daily Flash, Exco, and PPT outputs. The result was a repeatable daily view of P&L and NBI against budget for management decision-making.”
