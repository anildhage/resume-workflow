# FR Y-15 and FR 2052a Regulatory Reporting

**Organization:** Societe Generale Investment Banking  
**Period:** 04/2023 - Present  
**Role:** Business Analyst / Technical BA / data analyst

## Business context
- The role supported U.S. regulatory reporting for systemic-risk and liquidity monitoring at Societe Generale.
- The work involved data analysis, ETL support, issue triage, and controlled change delivery across a hybrid Bangalore and Montreal team.

## Problem or objective
- FR Y-15 and FR 2052a required complete, reconciled, and traceable data within tight reporting windows.
- The objective was to investigate anomalies, validate mappings, and drive permanent ETL or process fixes before reporting deadlines.

## Responsibilities and contributions
- Acted as the technical BA between U.S. business users and technical teams.
- Focused on journal balances, balance-sheet data, GL data, and related financial metrics.
- Investigated inconsistent report values, missing or late source data, incorrect balances, and unexplained movements.
- Used SQL to compare report outputs against raw GL and journal entries.
- Checked source arrival, processing status, mapping gaps, currency or FX issues, and possible double-counting.
- Explained whether issues were caused by upstream delays, mappings, process failures, or expected business events.
- Confirmed blocking issues before reporting cycles.
- Drove ETL, mapping, and calculation changes through DEV, UAT, sign-off, production deployment, and post-release validation.
- Supported liquidity data checks for cash flows, positions, collateral, and maturity-bucket mappings in FR 2052a.

## Tools, platforms, and systems
- SQL
- Python
- MS SQL Server
- FARMS ETL
- Autosys
- APIs, CSV files, and other source feeds
- SG Markets, MicroStrategy, and Power BI

## Outcomes and value
- Improved data completeness, reconciliation, and traceability for regulated financial reporting.
- Supported business sign-off with evidence-based investigation and controlled change delivery.
- Helped maintain reliable reporting for systemic-risk and liquidity monitoring.
- Reduced the risk of reporting issues caused by mapping, data-quality, or process failures.
