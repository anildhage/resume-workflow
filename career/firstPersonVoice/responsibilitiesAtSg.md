# Responsibilities at Societe Generale Investment Banking

**Organization:** Societe Generale Investment Banking  
**Period:** 04/2023 - Present  
**Role:** Business Analyst / Data Engineer / Reporting and Automation Specialist

## Business context
- The work spans business analysis, data engineering, ETL development, reporting, and production support.
- The objective is to turn business requirements into reliable data workflows and reporting outputs while coordinating across technical and business stakeholders.

## Problem or objective
- Reporting and operational data flows needed to be reliable, traceable, and executable across multiple systems and stakeholders.
- The goal was to build scalable, controlled workflows that support regulatory reporting, data quality, and production support.

## Responsibilities and contributions
- Co-developed Python-based ETL frameworks for scalable data transformation on Azure AKS.
- Integrated diverse data sources including APIs, database tables, database views, files, and Azure Blob Storage into complex transformation pipelines.
- Modernized legacy MS SQL processes by migrating them toward Azure SQL and replacing legacy scheduling patterns with Airflow.
- Built and maintained transformation logic in SQL and Python so analysis and business outputs remained repeatable.
- Supported FR Y-15 and FR 2052a regulatory reporting through data investigation, root-cause analysis, and controlled change delivery.
- Implemented GitHub-centered CI/CD workflows and deployment automation to support release readiness.
- Used GitHub Copilot to accelerate documentation, code understanding, refactoring, and legacy adaptation for cloud architecture.
- Worked with technical and business teams to support UAT, stakeholder sign-off, and production readiness.
