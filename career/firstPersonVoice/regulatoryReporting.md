# Regulatory Reporting

**Organization:** Societe Generale Investment Banking  
**Period:** 04/2023 - Present  
**Role:** Technical BA / data analyst / reporting specialist

## Business context
- FR Y-15 and FR 2052a are U.S. regulatory reporting streams focused on systemic-risk and liquidity monitoring.
- The work requires clear understanding of source feeds, control logic, mapping quality, and reporting windows.

## Problem or objective
- Reporting figures could be incorrect because of delayed files, mapping gaps, data-quality issues, or process failures.
- The objective was to investigate and resolve issues before reporting deadlines while ensuring traceability to source data.

## Responsibilities and contributions
- Acted as the technical BA between U.S. business users and technical teams.
- Focused on journal balances, balance-sheet data, GL data, and other financial metrics feeding FR Y-15.
- Compared report outputs with raw GL and journal entries using SQL.
- Investigated missing or late source data, incorrect balances, unexplained movements, mapping gaps, FX issues, and possible double-counting.
- Explained whether issues were caused by upstream delays, mapping errors, process failures, or expected business events.
- Supported liquidity reporting under FR 2052a by checking cash flows, positions, collateral, maturity-bucket logic, and product mappings.
- Drove ETL, mapping, and calculation changes through DEV, UAT, sign-off, production deployment, and post-release validation.

## Tools, platforms, and systems
- SQL
- Python
- MS SQL Server
- Autosys
- ETL pipelines
- GL and transaction data sources
- SG Markets, MicroStrategy, and Power BI

## Outcomes and value
- Improved issue investigation and root-cause analysis for regulatory data quality.
- Helped maintain complete, traceable, and timely reporting within reporting windows.
- Reduced risk of incorrect systemic-risk and liquidity outputs through stronger controls and validation.

## Evidence or summary
- “I support the Fed’s systemic-risk reporting for SG’s U.S. operations through FR Y-15, focusing on balance-sheet, GL, and exposure data used to monitor GSIB risk profiles and capital surcharges.”
- “I also support liquidity-risk reporting under FR 2052a, ensuring that detailed cash flows, funding, and collateral data are correctly captured across maturity buckets so supervisors can assess liquidity resilience under stress.”
