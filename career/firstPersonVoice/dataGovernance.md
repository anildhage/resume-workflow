# Data Governance

**Organization:** Societe Generale Investment Banking  
**Period:** 04/2023 - Present  
**Role:** Business Analyst / reporting control specialist

## Business context
- Regulatory and management-reporting data must be accurate, traceable, and compliant with banking controls.
- Governance is critical when data moves from source systems through ETL and into financial or regulatory reports.

## Problem or objective
- Missing feeds, incorrect balances, mapping errors, and inconsistent definitions can threaten reporting reliability.
- The objective was to maintain front-to-back data quality, traceability, and controlled change management for reporting outputs.

## Responsibilities and contributions
- Investigated missing feeds, incorrect balances, and mapping issues and drove permanent fixes instead of temporary workarounds.
- Traced FR Y-15, FR 2052a, and PMPI figures back to GL, P&L, and transactional source data through SQL and ETL flows.
- Supported pre-report sanity checks, reconciliations, explanations, and business sign-off.
- Managed changes through DEV, UAT, approvals, production deployment, and post-deployment integrity checks.
- Worked with stakeholders to keep KPI definitions consistent for P&L, NBI, DTD, MTD, QTD, YTD, budgets, and variances.

## Tools, platforms, and systems
- SQL
- ETL pipelines
- GL and transactional source data
- Regulatory reporting workflows
- Reporting controls and sign-off processes

## Outcomes and value
- Improved end-to-end traceability and reliability of regulated and management-reporting data.
- Reduced the risk of recurring reporting issues by addressing root causes rather than symptoms.
- Strengthened governance around quality checks, reconciliation, documentation, and sign-off.

## Evidence or summary
- “Although my title is Business Analyst, a large part of my role is data governance for regulatory and performance reporting. I make sure the data feeding FR Y-15, FR 2052a, and PMPI is complete, reconciled, and traceable end to end. I work from source-system loads through ETL to final reports, and I coordinate with business users on controls, documentation, and sign-off before reporting.”
