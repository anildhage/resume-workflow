# Common Data Platform and Tooling

**Organization:** Societe Generale Investment Banking  
**Period:** 04/2023 - Present  
**Role:** Data and reporting specialist

## Business context
- The platform supports regulatory and management-reporting workflows across GL, journal, transactional, and performance data.
- Reliability depends on end-to-end monitoring of source feeds, transformations, validations, and reporting outputs.

## Problem or objective
- Data can be technically generated but still be incomplete or inaccurate if upstream feeds or mapping logic fail.
- The objective is to maintain a controlled operational flow from source data through transformation, validation, and reporting.

## Responsibilities and contributions
- Used MS SQL Server to explore balances, journal data, transactional records, and reporting datasets.
- Worked with FARMS ETL to support sourcing, transformation, validation, and loading for FR Y-15, FR 2052a, and PMPI.
- Monitored Autosys batch dependencies, calendars, ingestion, transformations, and alerts for failed or long-running jobs.
- Supported dashboards and ad-hoc analysis through SG Markets, MicroStrategy, and Power BI.
- Used SQL, Python, and Unix shell scripting for reconciliation, automation, and data-quality checks.
- Applied business-calendar logic for U.S. holidays, month-end and quarter-end cut-offs, and reporting windows.

## Tools, platforms, and systems
- MS SQL Server
- FARMS ETL
- Autosys
- SQL
- Python
- Unix shell scripting
- SG Markets
- MicroStrategy
- Power BI

## Outcomes and value
- Improved trust in daily and regulatory reporting by validating the full data flow from source to output.
- Reduced issues caused by incomplete feeds, delayed processing, or mapping gaps.
- Increased reliability and operational visibility across reporting and batch orchestration.

## Evidence or summary
- “I work across the full reporting flow: source files and systems, SQL data stores, ETL transformations, batch scheduling, validation, and reporting outputs. I use SQL to reconcile and investigate, Python to automate and extend checks, and operational tools such as Autosys and Unix to monitor and support reliable daily processing.”
