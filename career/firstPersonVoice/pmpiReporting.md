# PMPI Performance Management and P&L Reporting

**Organization:** Societe Generale Investment Banking  
**Period:** 01/2026 - 06/2026  
**Role:** Lead BA / data transformation developer

## Business context
- PMPI supported daily and management reporting across MARK, GLBA, and GTPS.
- The work focused on translating business needs into repeatable daily P&L and NBI reporting against budget.

## Problem or objective
- Management needed earlier visibility into performance and variance before month-end close.
- The objective was to build standardized DTD, MTD, QTD, and YTD calculations and automate daily reporting outputs.

## Responsibilities and contributions
- Worked on daily, MTD, QTD, and YTD P&L and NBI views for MARK, GLBA, GTPS, and sub-business units.
- Translated management-reporting needs into data models, metric definitions, and report layouts.
- Owned end-to-end FARMS ETL pipelines handling daily feed ingestion, calculations, validation, and aggregation.
- Implemented budget proration, average-balance logic, and variance calculations against budget and prior periods.
- Checked feed timeliness and completeness, and handled late submissions, reruns, corrections, and business restructures.
- Worked with a UI developer on dashboards and end-of-day PPT outputs.
- Answered questions about performance versus prior periods or budget and adjusted logic as required.

## Tools, platforms, and systems
- FARMS ETL
- SQL
- Python
- Daily business-unit feeds and reporting data marts
- SG Markets and management reporting channels

## Outcomes and value
- Improved management visibility into performance before month-end close.
- Standardized calculations across business units and reduced manual reporting effort.
- Increased consistency and repeatability in daily P&L and NBI reporting.
- Helped identify under-performance or favorable variance earlier.

## Evidence or summary
- “I worked on PMPI as a lead BA and data-transformation developer. I turned management requirements into standardized DTD, MTD, QTD, YTD, budget-proration, and variance calculations, then integrated daily business-unit feeds into repeatable management reports. This helped management monitor performance against budget and identify issues earlier.”
