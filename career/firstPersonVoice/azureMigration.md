# Azure Cloud Migration

**Organization:** Societe Generale Investment Banking  
**Period:** Ongoing under current workstream  
**Role:** Data and reporting modernization specialist

## Business context
- Regulatory and performance-reporting workloads were running in on-premises and legacy batch-based environments.
- The workstream was focused on improving scalability, cost efficiency, governance, and future analytics readiness through Azure-based modernization.

## Problem or objective
- On-premises environments created cost and operational constraints for growing data volumes and more frequent reporting.
- The objective was to migrate workloads while preserving business logic, reliability, and operational controls.

## Responsibilities and contributions
- Helped move regulatory and performance-reporting applications from on-premises MS SQL Server and legacy ETL patterns toward Azure-based architectures.
- Worked on preserving business logic and validating output continuity during migration.
- Supported replacement of legacy scheduling patterns and modernization of operational controls.
- Contributed to the modernization of reporting and governance capabilities in Azure.

## Tools, platforms, and systems
- Azure
- MS SQL Server
- ETL workloads
- Legacy reporting and scheduling systems
- Monitoring and governance tooling

## Outcomes and value
- Improved scalability for larger data volumes and more frequent reporting.
- Reduced infrastructure cost and operational overhead associated with on-premises systems.
- Strengthened governance, monitoring, and resilience through Azure-based controls.
- Increased readiness for cloud-native analytics and future AI use cases.

## Evidence or summary
- “I am involved in progressively migrating our FR Y-15, FR 2052a, and PMPI workloads from on-premises MS SQL and legacy schedulers to Azure-based services. The purpose is to improve scalability, reduce infrastructure costs, and use modern data-governance and monitoring capabilities while preserving the reliability and business logic of the existing reporting processes.”
